# Archivo documental del Patronato de Protección a la Mujer

**Biblioteca digital · Digital Archive**

Interactive documentary archive and research guide presenting digitised historical photographs, verified Spanish transcriptions, English contextual explanations, and links to related archival holdings in Spain concerning the *Patronato de Protección a la Mujer*.

## Contents

- **`index.html`** — Standalone digital library application:
  - **Documents:** 30 curated reading-route records and 141 total working-catalogue records with embedded photographic previews, Spanish titles (`lang="es"`), and verified/draft ES–EN transcriptions.
  - **Other archives:** Guide to 9 related archival collections across Spain with institutional links and thematic cross-references.
  - **About this edition:** Editorial methodology, transcription review standards, and technical inventory notes.
