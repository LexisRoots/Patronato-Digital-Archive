# Archivo documental del Patronato de Protección a la Mujer

**Biblioteca digital · Almu Self-Contained Preview Edition (v8.2)**

Self-contained digital library presenting **114 public historical records** from **Master Catalog v8.2** with direct static access to **215 upright JPG photographs**, **117 Spanish-only (ES) transcription and inventory files**, and **5 independent QA evidence reports** — requiring **zero Google Drive login**.

## Publication & Rights Summary

- **Public records included:** 114 of 115 catalogued units (93 complete ES transcriptions, 20 incomplete ES transcriptions, 1 `DESCRIBED_ONLY` inventory).
- **Strictly excluded (`RESTRICTED`):** `TOLEDO-NAMED-1966` and all named personal medical/psychiatric records (0 bytes in repository).
- **Withheld pending copyright resolution:** `PRINT-UNKW-INSTITUTION-04-10` (UN DESA 1956/57 brochure) — only the structural description (`DESCRIBED_ONLY`) is published; its 5 JPGs and full text are withheld pending UN rights clearance.
- **No external Drive dependency:** Every photograph, Spanish transcription (`viewer.html`), QA report, and `MASTER_CATALOG_PATRONATO_ALMU_v8.2.csv` is served statically.
