## 3. Short explanation (EN)

Dated 29 February 1956, Circular no. 46 addresses the jurisdictional boundary between women's prisons (*cárceles de mujeres*) and the institutions of the *Patronato de Protección a la Mujer* under the Law of 20 December 1952 (*Ley Orgánica* of the Patronato). The national leadership notes two recurring situations involving underage young women (*jóvenes menores de edad*):
1. Minors detained by governmental (police/civil governor) authority for conduct deemed offensive to "public morality" (*hechos que atañen a su pública honestidad*) were increasingly being sent to regular women's prisons instead of being placed at the disposal of the Patronato for internment in one of its institutions, as required by the Law of 20 December 1952.
2. Underage young women imprisoned for other offenses who were about to be released (*al poner en libertad*) were considered at "risk of corruption" upon release, making it desirable to transfer them directly to the provincial boards of the Patronato at the moment of their discharge from prison.

To ensure strict enforcement of the 1952 Organic Law, the circular instructs provincial boards to intensify their vigilance and maintain close liaison with both governmental authorities and the directors of prisons holding female minors.

---

## 4. Key obligations, prohibitions, conditions, and deadlines (EN)

- **Obligations for Provincial Boards:**
  - Exercise strict vigilance to ensure full enforcement of the Patronato's Organic Law (*Ley de 20 de diciembre de 1952*).
  - Maintain close contact (*estrecho contacto*) with governmental authorities (*autoridades gubernativas*) and directors of penitentiary establishments (*directores de establecimientos penitenciarios*) where underage young women are held.
  - Ensure that underage young women detained by governmental authorities for matters of "public morality" are placed at the disposal of the Patronato for internment in an appropriate institution rather than sent to women's prisons.
  - Arrange for underage female prisoners being released after serving sentences for other offenses to be placed at the disposal of the provincial boards if deemed in "danger of corruption."
- **Prohibition / Legal rule cited:** Underage young women detained governmentally for morality-related conduct must not be sent to regular women's prisons (*cárceles de mujeres*) in lieu of Patronato internment under the Law of 20 December 1952.
- **Deadlines:** **None** (continuous institutional coordination requirement).
