Sin embargo, estas dificultades no deben paralizar nuestros esfuerzos, sino acuciarnos a todos para vencerlos con el sacrificio de todos. La Obra bien merece la pena.

## 3. Short explanation (EN)

Issued on 18 June 1956, Circular no. 48 follows the enactment of the Decree-Law of 3 March 1956 (*Decreto-Ley de 3 de marzo de 1956*, which abolished tolerated brothels in Spain and entrusted the *Patronato de Protección a la Mujer* with the "regeneration and re-education" of women coming under its purview). Emphasizing the increased institutional responsibility resulting from the Decree-Law, the Junta Nacional calls for the revitalization of provincial and local boards and reiterates three mandatory internal operating rules:
1. The Standing Committee (*Comisión Permanente*) of each board must meet regularly and **at least twice a month**.
2. Repeated non-attendance by board members (*Vocales*) must be treated as an absolute inability to collaborate and reported immediately to the Civil Governor (*Gobernador Civil*) so a replacement can be proposed.
3. Administrative staff, *Celadoras* (female supervisors/wardens), and attached personnel must strictly observe the working hours set out in Circular no. 19, and boards must report any compliance deficiencies in full detail to the Junta Nacional.

The circular acknowledges that budget constraints limit provincial boards' activities, but urges them to overcome financial shortages through personal sacrifice.

---

## 4. Key obligations, prohibitions, conditions, and deadlines (EN)

- **Mandatory meeting frequency (Rule 1º):** The Standing Committee (*Comisión Permanente*) of each provincial/local board must meet **at least twice a month** (*«como mínimo dos veces al mes»*) to review results, adopt resolutions, or submit proposals to the Junta Nacional.
- **Sanction for absenteeism of board members (Rule 2º):** Repeated non-attendance (*no asistencia reiterada*) by board members (*Vocales*) must be reported **immediately** (*inmediatamente*) to the Civil Governor (*Gobernador Civil*) for replacement (*propuesta de sustitución*).
- **Staff working hours and reporting obligation (Rule 3º):**
  - Administrative staff, *Celadoras*, and attached personnel must strictly comply with the working hours established in **Circular no. 19**.
  - Provincial boards must report in full detail (*con todo detalle*) to the Junta Nacional any deficiency in fulfilling these working-hour obligations.
- **Deadlines / Frequency:** Minimum of **2 meetings per month** for the *Comisión Permanente*; **immediate** notification to the Civil Governor upon repeated absenteeism of *Vocales*.

## 5. Uwagi o nieczytelności, pisowni historycznej i kompletności

1. **Stan czytelności:** Maszynopis powielany jest w pełni czytelny. W nagłówku `CIRCULAR NUMERO 48.` oraz w słowach `La Comisión` (pkt 1º) i podpisie dolnym odbicie farby z matrycy jest nieco bledsze, lecz wszystkie litery tekstu normatywnego są jednoznacznie rozpoznawalne. Podpis pod `JEFE DE LOS SERVICIOS` jest odbity jedynie szczątkowo (nieczytelny). Pole nazwy prowincji po słowie `en` jest puste (`[en blanco]`).
2. **Oryginalne literówki maszynowe i cechy pisowni (zachowane wiernie):**
   - W akapicie 1, w. 2–3: **`cenesitamos [sic: necesitamos]`** (`sobre cuya importancia no cenesi- / tamos insistir.`) — przestawka literowa (metateza) na matrycy maszynowej;
   - W akapicie 1, w. 14: `tambien` (bez akcentu) oraz `con` nadpisane na błędnym `don`;
   - W akapicie 2, w. 3 oraz w pkt 2º, w. 3: akcenty grawisowe `aplicàndose` i `podrà`;
   - W pkt 3º, w. 4: przebita litera `t` w spacji między słowami `con` i `todo` (`con[t]todo`).
