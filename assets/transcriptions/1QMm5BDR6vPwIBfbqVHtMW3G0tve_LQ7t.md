## 3. Short explanation (EN)

This national circular instructs provincial boards of the *Patronato de Protección a la Mujer* to compile and send a comprehensive retrospective activity report (*Memoria*) covering the period from 1941 to late 1963. The data was requested for a book documenting the institution's work since its founding, intended as a contribution to the regime's 1964 commemorative exhibition *"25 años de paz"* (with the words *"Magna Exposición"* struck out on the typescript).

The circular specifies fifteen quantitative and descriptive reporting categories: board meetings held, case files opened, admissions (*internamientos*), releases (*externamientos*), case dismissals (*sobreseimientos*), placements in provincial institutions and *Casas de Familia*, young women under *Vigilancia Tutelar*, a breakdown of internments by initiating authority or petitioner (judicial authority, governmental authority, parents/guardians, or self-petition), police reports on young women, boarding houses, suspect houses, runaways, transfers, arrests and *Ballets*, judicial complaints filed by the board, and post-intervention outcomes (marriages, entry into religious institutes, job placements, and re-internments due to conduct). As historical evidence, the document reveals the central administration's statistical categories at the end of 1963, not actual provincial totals.

---

## 4. Key obligations, prohibitions, conditions, and deadlines (EN)

- **Obligation:** Each provincial board must prepare and submit a comprehensive activity report (*Memoria*) covering its work from 1941 to the date of the circular, structured around the 15 enumerated statistical categories.
- **Addressee / Responsible official:** First Vice-President of the Provincial Board (*Sr. Vicepresidente 1º de la Junta Provincial*).
- **Mandatory breakdown (Item 8º):** Internment totals must be disaggregated into four sub-categories: (a) judicial order, (b) governmental order, (c) request of parents or guardians, and (d) self-request (*a petición propia*).
- **Prohibitions / Sanctions:** None stated in the circular.
- **Deadline:** The report must reach the Junta Nacional in Madrid **by 1 January 1964** (*«Esta Memoria deberá hallarse en nuestro poder el 1º de Enero de 1.964»*).
