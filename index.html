Editorial method and source integrity

About this edition.

This page describes the work behind the library. It is intentionally separate from the document-reading experience.

How the documents were reconstructed

Source photographs were catalogued, repeat captures recognised, page sequences compared, and original papers distinguished from different editions, copies and annexes. Decisions were grounded in matching text, dates, numbering and contents pages; unresolved joins remain unresolved.

Source images are preserved unmodified. The current library is a working reconstruction rather than a critical edition of every document.

Transcriptions and reviews (v8.2)

All canonical transcriptions in this edition are faithful Spanish-only (ES) transcriptions hosted directly alongside the library with zero Google Drive login required. Source completeness is strictly separated from transcription completeness and independent QA status.

Counts are not interchangeable: photographs, physical sheets, historical documents and editorial catalogue entries are different things.
